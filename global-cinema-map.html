⚖️ National Quality Gap

Violin plots showing the distribution of movie quality (IMDb ratings) for each country, arranged by their economic inequality (Gini coefficient). This reveals whether economic inequality translates to "two-tier" cinema systems.

Research Question: Is there a relationship between economic inequality in a country and the quality gap in its cinema?

📊 How to Read This Visualization

🎻 Violin Shapes: Show the distribution of movie ratings for each country. Wider areas = more movies at that rating level.

🟥→🟦 Colors: Countries are colored by their Gini coefficient (economic inequality). Red = high inequality, Blue = low inequality.

📦 Box Plots: White boxes show quartiles (25%-75%), black lines show medians, red dots show mean ratings.

📈 Arrangement: Countries are ordered from highest inequality (left) to lowest inequality (right).

Key Question: Do countries with higher economic inequality show wider gaps in movie quality? Look for wider violin shapes in red countries vs. narrow shapes in blue countries.
