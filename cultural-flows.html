🎬 Research Question: How does cinematic influence flow between countries?

This visualization maps the cultural influence flows in global cinema. Producer countries (left, red) create and export cinematic content, while consumer countries (right, teal) receive and are influenced by this content. The thickness of flows indicates influence strength, calculated from movie popularity, ratings, and cultural similarity.

📋 Methodology

  • Influence Score: Combination of movie count, average votes, and ratings
  • Flow Strength: Producer influence × Consumer receptivity × Genre similarity × Regional bonus
  • Cultural Producers: Top 8 countries with highest influence scores
  • Cultural Consumers: Next 12 countries in the ranking
  • Color Coding: Flows colored by producer's dominant genre
